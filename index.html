a little about us

hello there!

siteforsite is a place where your little idea turns into a real, working website, without the scary agency price tag.

We're a small team of three college students from Delhi University who love making pretty, useful things for the internet. You tell us what you need (a portfolio for internship season, a link-in-bio page for your small business, a surprise page for your best friend's birthday), and we design it, build it and put it live for you.

You get exactly what you wanted, we get to do what we love, and it costs less than a pizza. That's the whole idea.
